Why Pure SVG Icons Win

1. Zero Visual Bloat
  • No pill bubbles: Eliminates clumsy border bubbles that waste 20px+ of horizontal space each.
  • High density & clarity: 10+ core operations fit comfortably along a smooth, friction-free horizontal track.
2. Immediate 1-Tap Execution
  • Tap Rotate, Crop, Dup, or Delete directly from the ribbon without opening sub-menus.
  • Micro-labels below each icon ensure 100% clarity and zero guessing.
3. Touch Target Compliance
  • Each icon item has an exact 44 × 40px hitbox with tactile active press feedback.
