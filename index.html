Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our local-first offline architecture, cryptographic licensing, and privacy guarantees.

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our support team

Why is FileKit a $14 one-time payment instead of a monthly subscription?

Traditional PDF and file web tools process your files on cloud server clusters, incurring heavy recurring compute, bandwidth, and storage bills that they pass on as monthly subscriptions. FileKit runs 100% locally on your computer's CPU using WebAssembly. Because our ongoing cloud compute cost is zero, we pass the savings directly to you with a single lifetime purchase.

Does FileKit really work completely offline?

Yes, absolutely. Once loaded in your browser or installed as a standalone desktop app, every single tool (PDF merging, compression, OCR, conversion, and format editing) executes strictly inside your device's sandbox. You can disconnect your Wi-Fi, enable Airplane mode, or work inside secure air-gapped workstations without interruption.

What happens if I change my computer or use multiple devices?

Your license key is permanent and personal to you. You can activate it across your personal workstations, including macOS, Windows, Linux, and mobile browsers. If you upgrade to a new computer, just re-enter your license key to unlock Pro features instantly.

Are my files or document contents ever uploaded or logged?

Never. Your files never leave your device's memory. FileKit has no document upload endpoints, no remote database, and no telemetry tracking your file contents. Your sensitive financial, legal, and confidential documents remain 100% private to you.

Are updates included, or will I be asked to pay again later?

All future updates, performance upgrades, and new tool additions are included in your lifetime license at no additional charge. There are no surprise renewal fees, tiered upgrade paywalls, or hidden recurring charges.

How does license key activation work without tracking me?

FileKit licenses are verified using an offline mathematical cryptographic checksum directly within your browser or app. It doesn't send telemetry pings, install background daemons, or store tracking cookies. It just unlocks your software cleanly.
